# Governance

Anasemble currently uses a maintainer-led model. The repository owner is the
release, security and governance maintainer and may delegate bounded areas as the
community grows.

Routine decisions are made through reviewed pull requests. Material protocol,
trust-boundary, compatibility, licensing, governance or implementation-language
changes require an ADR and explicit maintainer approval. Security response may
remain private until coordinated disclosure is safe. Releases require the gates
in `docs/RELEASE.md`; no contributor can infer release authority from a merged
change.

Maintainers are expected to explain material rejections, disclose conflicts of
interest, preserve claim boundaries and apply the code of conduct consistently.
Future maintainer appointments require sustained high-quality contributions,
sound review judgment, responsible security handling and explicit owner approval.
Removal may follow inactivity, repeated policy violations or loss of trust, with
private handling where safety or privacy requires it.
