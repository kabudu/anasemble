# Risk Register

| ID | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Owner | Trigger / evidence | Treatment and cadence |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|---|---|
| R1 | Contribution collides with synthesis/self-construction work | Medium | High | Research owner | closer paper, product, or patent; M3 search remains inconclusive | prohibit novelty claims; refresh before any paper or public opening |
| R2 | Oracle or contract incompleteness | High | Critical | Semantics owner | unsafe certification or uncovered effect | finite DSL, mandatory negatives, refusal; stop on one unsafe certification; every milestone |
| R3 | Shared survivor fault | Medium | Critical | Security owner | domains share control or key material | issuer-domain binding and adversarial tests; optional independent attestation can strengthen assurance |
| R4 | Trace poisoning or overfitting | High | High | Evaluation owner | held-out disagreement or training leakage | held-out and metamorphic checks; retain negative rows; every corpus change |
| R5 | Synthesizer/checker common bug | Medium | Critical | Assurance owner | differential or reproduction disagreement | separate semantics and candidate parser; independent implementation review remains open |
| R6 | Hidden state or side effects | High | Critical | Architecture owner | component needs undeclared I/O or external state | explicit finite state and denied imports; refuse scope expansion without a new model |
| R7 | Reconstruction costs exceed backups or centralized contracts | High | High | Product decision owner | current evidence is 6.75 times artifact bytes and centralized contracts match recovery | continue production engineering only where the distinct failure scope is valuable; remeasure every adapter and corpus |
| R8 | Name, package, patent, or trademark collision | High | High | Legal owner | active 2026 `AINSEMBLE` software application is phonetically close; exact technical market overlap is not established | retain the publication-date screen, make no clearance claim, and block commercialisation, paid services, filing, merchandising, or major promotion pending professional advice |
| R9 | Wasmi, compiler, dependency, or host compromise | Low | Critical | Security owner | advisory, sandbox escape, reproducibility mismatch | pinned Rust and lockfile, audit, empty imports, internal adversarial review; optional external review post-release |
| R10 | Kernel evidence is mistaken for production assurance | High | Critical | Claims owner | unqualified safe, recovery, novel, or production wording | production roadmap gates, private repository, no release authority; review every release surface |
| R11 | Container or orchestrator controls fail to enforce the declared activation boundary | Low | Critical | Runtime owner | sandbox escape, policy-ignoring CNI, admission mutation, split-brain activation, or secret disclosure | pinned immutable artifacts, zero-egress profile, direct Docker adversarial tests, Kubernetes lease/switch drills, explicit CNI prerequisite; every runtime or cluster change |
| R12 | Durable operations duplicate, lose, or expose recovery work after interruption | Low | Critical | Operations owner | broken audit chain, concurrent store mutation, early lease recovery, queue over-admission, or sensitive support output | atomic sealed records, create-new lock, explicit clock, attempts, backpressure, allowlisted support schema, restart and sustained tests; every operations change |
| R13 | Product-readiness evidence is mistaken for release or broad platform authority | Medium | High | Release owner | tag, deployment, public claim, unsupported platform, or package publication without gate | `publish = false`, compatibility manifest, retained preview only, explicit release authority and licence gate; every release decision |
