# Publication-date name diligence

## Scope and result

This point-in-time search was refreshed on 2026-08-11 before public source
opening. It covered exact, case-folded, hyphenated, and similar forms across
general web results, GitHub, crates.io, public product surfaces, and accessible
US, UK, and EU trademark search surfaces. It is a screening record, not legal
advice, trademark clearance, patent freedom to operate, or a worldwide search.

No exact `Anasemble` disaster-recovery product, GitHub project, or crates.io
package was found in the searched results. The repository and product continue
to use the coined spelling `Anasemble` and the Semantic Fit mark.

## Material similar mark

The refresh found `AINSEMBLE`, used by an active music and artistic-production
software product and covered by a 2026 US word-mark application in international
classes 009 and 042 (USPTO serial 99636640). The spelling and market purpose are
different, but pronunciation and broad software classes are close enough to
create a real confusion and opposition risk. An older `AASEMBLE` US application
for software deployment and management was abandoned in 2017.

Public source opening may proceed only as a bounded open-source project decision,
with no statement that the name is cleared or registrable. Commercialisation,
paid services, trademark registration, merchandising, or material marketing
spend remains blocked pending professional trademark advice covering the target
jurisdictions and the `AINSEMBLE` application.

## Reconsideration rule

Repeat the search before any commercial launch, package-registry reservation,
domain campaign, trademark filing, or major identity investment. Stop public
promotion and seek professional advice if an exact mark, a closer technical
product, an objection, or user confusion appears.
