# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Anasemble contributors must make participation respectful, technically honest
and safe. Welcome people of different backgrounds and experience; critique ideas
and evidence rather than people; disclose uncertainty; respect privacy; and stop
harassment, intimidation, discrimination, sexualised conduct, threats, doxxing
and sustained disruption.

## Scope and enforcement

This standard applies in repository spaces and when representing the project.
Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub's
private reporting channel once enabled. Until then, use the contact method on the
owner's GitHub profile without including sensitive third-party information.

Maintainers will minimise disclosure, hear affected parties, preserve relevant
evidence and take proportionate action. Responses may include a private warning,
content removal, temporary participation limits or permanent exclusion. Retaliation
against a good-faith reporter is prohibited. Immediate safety concerns should be
directed to the appropriate local authority rather than the project.
